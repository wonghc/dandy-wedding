Accomodations

Black Canyon Inn

The venue is hosted in the Black Canyon Inn, a series of buildings each containing a couple of condos. Condos require a 2-night minimum stay. On the same property but a little further away is the Black Canyon Resort, a traditional hotel style setup. Hotel guests receive complimentary golf carts rides ferrying guests living on the property to and from the venue on the day of the event.

Holiday Inn Estes Park

The Holiday Inn is a 5-minute drive away from the venue, and has amenities such as complimentary bike rental, indoor pool, game room, and gas outdoor firepit. Dog friendly. Some rates include breakfast.
